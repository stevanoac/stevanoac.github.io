apcsp project

in 12th grade i took ap computer science principles (apcsp). my project was a simple image to ascii art converter.

images soon™

features:

  • input any image (supported most image types)
  • then it would turn that image into ascii art
  • the ascii art would be generated into a notepad
  • it also allowed for you to compress the ascii art
  • so you could compress a 1920x1080 image by 50% into the notepad
