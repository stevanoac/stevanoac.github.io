"smart app"

"smart app" was a personal self hosted website that I made. I would display this website on a modded amazon fire 7 tablet on my desk. the "smart app" displayed info about my cpu, gpu and ram. as well as it would detect the battery of my mouse and keyboard. it would also display what i was listening to on spotify, and had controls to rewind, play, skip and volume. It also never really hade a name, so I guess I just decided on "smart app".

This project was made in 2026.

some features:

  • hardware info
  • mouse & keyboard info
  • light/dark mode
  • custom graphs
  • spotify controller
