nice club

nice club (formerly known as evil gang) was a friend group consisting of 4 original members. we had originally planned on making merchandise and dropping music. we made some music, but the merchandise never went any further than concepts.

we're all still friends, but the merchandise never went beyond a concept because we were just a bunch of broke kids. we also decided, that maybe being called "evil gang" wasn't the best thing. so we unoffically rebranded to nice club.

we do not claim any instrumenals, beats, sound effects as our own. nice club only made the lyrics and vocals. this website does not make any commission or money, and we never have from our merchandise or music.

This started around in 2022.
