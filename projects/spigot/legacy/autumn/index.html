autumn

Autumn is my interpretation of a professional and feature-rich spigot practice plugin.

I created this project in 2025 and it's for the most part finished. although like most of my other projects, i always find new things i want to add later.

It was made alongside Mango, and they both integrate seamlessly with each other.

some features:

  • duels
  • arenas and arena resetting integration
  • arena rating system
  • kits and kit types (bedwars, sumo, stick fight, and more)
  • custom specatator mode
  • party fights, party splits, party events, hcf
  • events (sumo, oitc, tournament, and more)
  • custom scoreboard
  • leaderboards, settings, cosmetics
  • integration with mango
  • economy
  • mongodb integration
  • and so, so much more
